2. DANH CHO KHACH HANG
================================================================================

2.1. XEM & MUA GIAO TRINH [OK]
------------------------------
Cach dung:
1. Vao trang chu
2. Loc theo danh muc: AI, STEM, Phuong phap, Ky nang
3. Click san pham de xem chi tiet
4. Nhap thong tin: Ho ten, Email, SDT
5. Thanh toan qua QR (hien thi ma QR)
6. Doi Admin xac nhan va gui tai lieu qua email

Luu y: Quy trinh thanh toan la THU CONG - Admin can kiem tra va gui file.

2.2. TU SOAN KHBD [OK]
----------------------
Dia chi: /generator.html -> Tab KHBD

Cach dung:
1. Nhap API Key (do ban cung cap)
2. Bam "Kiem tra" de xac nhan key hoat dong
3. (Tuy chon) Upload file sach PDF/anh
4. Chon: Cap hoc -> Lop -> Mon -> Bo sach -> Bai hoc
5. Bam "Tao KHBD"
6. Doi AI soan (1-3 phut)
7. Bam "Xuat Word" de tai file

Mon hoc ho tro:
- THCS (Lop 6-9): Toan, Ngu van, Tieng Anh, KHTN, Lich su, Dia ly, GDCD, 
                   Tin hoc, Cong nghe, Am nhac, My thuat, HDTN
- THPT (Lop 10-12): Tuong tu + Vat ly, Hoa hoc, Sinh hoc rieng

Bo sach: Ket noi tri thuc, Chan troi sang tao, Canh dieu

2.3. TU SOAN SKKN [OK]
----------------------
Dia chi: /generator.html -> Tab SKKN

Cach dung:
1. Nhap API Key
2. Bam "Kiem tra"
3. Dien thong tin: Vai tro, Mon hoc, Cap hoc, De tai
4. (Tuy chon) Cau hinh: So tu 5000-20000, So giai phap 3-7, Deep Research
5. Bam "Tao SKKN"
6. Doi AI soan (3-10 phut tuy do dai)
7. Xuat Word khi hoan thanh

Cau truc SKKN: Theo QD 658/QD-SGDDT
- Phan I: Mo dau (ly do, muc dich, doi tuong, phuong phap)
- Phan II: Noi dung (co so ly luan, thuc trang, giai phap)
- Phan III: Ket qua, ket luan, kien nghi

2.4. TAO DE THI [OK]
--------------------
Dia chi: /exam-generator.html

Cach dung:
1. Nhap API Key
2. Chon Mon hoc (12 mon)
3. Chon Lop (6-12)
4. Chon Loai de: 15 phut, 45 phut, Giua ky, Cuoi ky
5. Nhap Noi dung kien thuc
6. Chon So ma de (1-4)
7. Bam "Tao de thi"
8. Tai: De thi Word + Dap an Word

Ma tran de: Theo Thong tu 22/2021
- Nhan biet, Thong hieu, Van dung, Van dung cao

2.5. LICH SU TAI LIEU [OK]
--------------------------
